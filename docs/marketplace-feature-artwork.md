## Final prompt

Create a flat 2D illustration PNG WITH A TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND (real alpha channel, background=transparent). This is an isolated cutout asset, NOT an image of a transparency checkerboard.
A compact collection of colorful filmmaking asset cards: one large navy filmstrip frame with a very simple flat peach sunset and blue ocean illustration, surrounded by smaller rounded blue and lavender tiles with a music note, waveform, Aa font glyph and a transition arrow. All drawings use flat solid color fills and crisp clean simple edges, like a high-quality modern editorial vector illustration. Straight-on front view, no perspective, no dimensional shading, no gradients, no 3D, no shadow.
Wide 2:1 balanced composition. Isolate the collection on EMPTY TRANSPARENT CANVAS. Absolutely no backdrop, no white rectangle, no gray grid, no checkerboard pattern, no floor or pedestal, no border, no watermark. Space between the separate tiles and around the whole collection must have alpha=0.
Intended use: RxFilmStudio marketplace announcement sheet, over both light and dark native macOS surfaces.
