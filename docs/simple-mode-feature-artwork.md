Generated with the built-in image generation tool for the **Simple mode**
What's New card. The transparent PNG is bundled as
`film-workflow/Assets.xcassets/WhatsNewSimpleModeGenerated.imageset/simple-mode.png`.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent hero illustration for the Simple mode What's New card in RxFilmStudio, a native macOS filmmaking app.
Primary request: Show how answering a few questions turns a user's website and footage into a first cut. Create a polished, compact editorial illustration: on the left a small warm-cream rounded questionnaire card, with two short navy placeholder lines and two large rounded choice chips, one selected with a cobalt checkmark; a small lavender conversation bubble overlaps its upper edge. A clean flowing cobalt arrow leads to a larger rounded navy film-preview panel on the right. The preview contains a beautiful simplified peach sunset over vivid cobalt ocean with cream reflections and navy coastal silhouettes. Beneath that preview, a short two-track timeline of cobalt and lavender clip blocks with one peach playhead. One small tilted footage thumbnail sits tucked behind the preview, and one small four-point lavender sparkle communicates creative assistance. Keep this as one cohesive, carefully balanced illustration with the film preview the dominant focal point.
Style/medium: high-quality flat 2D editorial illustration, vector-like crisp clean edges, bold deep navy outlines, softly rounded shapes, simple solid fills. Match a consistent native creative-app announcement illustration set.
Color palette: deep navy, vivid cobalt blue, soft lavender, peach, and warm cream.
Composition/framing: wide 2:1 canvas, balanced centered artwork with 8 percent clear outer margins; large simple shapes that remain legible at 384 by 192 points. Straight-on front view. Use the height well without cropping any part.
Background: actual transparent alpha channel; entirely empty outside and between isolated objects. PNG cutout suitable for both light and dark macOS surfaces.
Constraints: artwork only, no app screenshot, no card title, no words, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no background rectangle, no checkerboard, no floor or pedestal, no photorealism, no 3D, no perspective depth, no drop shadows, no gradients.
